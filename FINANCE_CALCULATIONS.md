# CareerCloset Finance Calculations

All figures below are planning assumptions unless labeled as a formula or target. The workspace contains no financial statements, completed rentals, customer interviews, or signed financing terms.

## Financing Ask and Illustrative Ownership

- Proposed financing ask: `$15,000`
- Proposed instrument: post-money SAFE
- Assumed valuation cap: `$135,000`
- Illustrative post-money amount at the cap: `$135,000 + $15,000 = $150,000`
- Illustrative ownership: `$15,000 / $150,000 = 10%`

This is a classroom scenario only. It is not an independent valuation, signed term sheet, or legal recommendation. Actual SAFE conversion can depend on the instrument's terms and later financing.

## Runway Scenario

- Assumed starting cash before the round: `$0`
- Proposed financing: `$15,000`
- Assumed average monthly net burn: `$2,500`
- Formula: `$15,000 / $2,500 per month = 6 months`

| Period | Opening cash | Financing | Assumed net burn | Ending cash |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Close | $0 | $15,000 | $0 | $15,000 |

| Month 2 | $12,500 | $0 | ($2,500) | $10,000 |
| Month 3 | $10,000 | $0 | ($2,500) | $7,500 |

The schedule assumes even monthly **net** burn, no unplanned costs, and no additional financing. It is not supported by operating history. The use-of-funds table is not added on top of this burn schedule; doing so would double-count the same round proceeds.

## Rental Unit Economics

- Assumed rental price: `$29`
- Assumed cleaning and handling cost: `$8` per rental
- Contribution before fixed costs: `$29 - $8 = $21` per rental
- Assumed semester membership: `$79`; margin not yet modeled
- Campus/employer sponsor revenue: possible, not committed or included in the contribution calculation

No customer price acceptance, cleaning cost, damage/loss rate, utilization, repeat rate, or sponsor willingness to pay has been observed. Do not present the assumed `$21` as profit; it excludes fixed costs and unmeasured costs.

## Six-Month Milestones and Definitions

- 30 completed student interviews: target
- 75 completed pilot rentals: target
- 1 paid campus or sponsor: target
- At least 80% on-time returns: target, calculated as rentals returned on time divided by rentals due
- At least 85% fit satisfaction: target, measured from post-rental responses
- Positive rental contribution: go/no-go gate, based on observed rental revenue less directly attributable cleaning, handling, and loss costs

Expansion requires the operating targets plus positive contribution. If a gate fails, pause inventory growth and revise or stop the pilot.
